# Walkable Bars & Restaurants  
## Downtown Arts District (18b) & Nearby

> **Scope:** The Arts District (18b) spans ~18 blocks around Main St, Charleston Blvd, and Commerce St. Most spots are within 5–10 minutes on foot, with a mix of craft cocktail lounges, dive bars, breweries, coffeehouses, and casual eateries.  

---

### Bars & Nightlife

- **Velveteen Rabbit** — Craft cocktails in a vintage, artsy lounge setting. *1218 S Main St.*  
- **Dark Sister** — Zodiac-inspired cocktails, candlelit witchy vibes. *1410 S Main St.*  
- **Stray Pirate** — Pirate-themed tiki / tropical cocktail bar. *1321 S Commerce St.*  
- **Liquid Diet** — Hidden alley bar with inventive drinks, open kitchen. *1415 S Commerce St, Ste 130.*  
- **18bin** — Arts Factory bar with small plates and drinks. *107 E Charleston Blvd, Ste 150.*  
- **ReBAR** — Dive bar + thrift shop, relaxed vibe, occasional tacos. *1225 S Main St.*  
- **Davy’s Locker** — Neighboring dive-style bar to ReBAR. *1247 S Main St.*  
- **Horse Trailer Hideout** — Converted trailer turned neighborhood bar. *1506 S Main St.*  
- **Echo (Vinyl Lounge “Echo”)** — Cocktails + vinyl listening lounge. *1301 S Main St #160.*  
- **Hard Hat Lounge** *(nearby, not technically 18b)* — One of Vegas’ oldest dive bars; burgers, sandwiches, occasional live music. *1675 S Industrial Rd.*  

---

### Food & Quick Bites

- **Esther’s Kitchen** — House-made pasta & Italian fare. *1130 S Casino Center Blvd, Ste 110.*  
- **Letty’s de Leticia’s Cocina** — Mexican street-food style eats. *807 S Main St.*  
- **Makers & Finders** — Latin café, brunch, and coffee bar. *1120 S Main St #110.*  
- **The Great Greek Mediterranean Grill** — Casual Mediterranean bowls and wraps. *801 S Main St, Suite 140.*  
- **Dona Maria Tamales** — Local staple for tamales and homestyle Mexican. *910 S Las Vegas Blvd.*  
- **The Pepper Club (English Hotel)** — Sushi + Asian fusion in boutique hotel. *921 S Main St.*  
- **Chicago Joe’s** — Old-school Italian comfort food. *820 S 4th St.*  
- **Good Pie** — NY & Detroit-style pizza, casual slices. *1212 S Main St, Ste 140.*  
- **Dig It! Coffee Co.** — Coffee, pastries, light bites. *1300 S Casino Center Blvd, Unit 110.*  
- **ReBAR (Birria Tacos)** — Birria tacos alongside bar offerings. *1225 S Main St.*  
- **The Stadium** — Sports bar with ballpark-style eats. *1508 S Main St.*  
- **Palate** — Americana bar + lounge food, modern yet approachable. *1301 S Main St #110.*  

---

### Sample Walking Route (≈35–45 min excluding stops)

1. Start with **Good Pie** for a slice.  
2. Stroll to **Letty’s de Leticia’s** for tacos (3–4 min).  
3. Coffee stop at **Makers & Finders** (2 min).  
4. Cocktails at **Dark Sister** (5 min).  
5. Pop into **Velveteen Rabbit** (2–3 min).  
6. Cross to **Stray Pirate** (3–5 min).  
7. Finish at **ReBAR / Davy’s Locker** (5 min).  
8. Optional: extend to **Hard Hat Lounge** (≈10–12 min walk / short rideshare).  

---

### Tips

- **First Friday** brings food trucks, art, and live music — streets close to cars, foot traffic is heavy.  
- The **English Hotel** anchors part of Main St with Pepper Club, providing a higher-end option.  
- Best to explore late afternoon → evening; many bars open after 4–5 pm.  
- Area is compact but still urban: sidewalks are good, but watch for crosswalks and traffic at Charleston.
