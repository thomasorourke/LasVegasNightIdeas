# Walkable Bars & Restaurants
## Chinatown (Las Vegas)

> **Scope:** Vegas’s Chinatown runs ~3 miles along Spring Mountain Rd (Rainbow Blvd → Valley View Blvd), a mile west of the Strip—dense, strip-mall style, very walkable by blocks/clusters.

### Quick walking clusters
- **Spring Mountain @ Arville–Polaris** — high-density food + nightlife within ~10–15 min on foot.
- **Center at Spring Mountain** — multiple plazas packed together.

### Picks (Food)
- **ShangHai Taste** — xiao long bao specialists; ~3,000 dumplings/day. *4266 W Spring Mountain Rd #104A.*  
- **Banana Leaf Café** — Malaysian staples (roti canai, nasi lemak). *4276 Spring Mountain Rd C101.*  
- **Hachi Japanese Yakitori Izakaya** — standout grilled skewers. *3410 S Jones Blvd.*  
- **Kame Omakase** — high-end, reservation-only sushi omakase (two nightly seatings). *3616 W Spring Mountain Rd Ste 103.*  
- **Chinatown Plaza (multi-option hub)** — 888 Korean BBQ, Capital Seafood, and more. *4255 Spring Mountain Rd.*  

### Picks (Bars & Night Spots)
- **The Golden Tiki** — destination tiki bar (open 24/7); *3939 Spring Mountain Rd.*  
- **The Sand Dollar Lounge (Spring Mountain)** — iconic live-music cocktail lounge; *3355 Spring Mountain Rd.*  
- **Más Por Favor Taqueria y Tequila** — taco shop with a hidden speakeasy in back; *3879 Spring Mountain Rd.*  

### Sample walk (≈35–45 min total, excluding stops)
- Start **ShangHai Taste** → **Banana Leaf** (3–5 min) → **Golden Tiki** (7–10 min) → **Sand Dollar Lounge** (12–15 min).  
- Side quest: pop into **Más Por Favor** (5–8 min from Golden Tiki).  

*All on/just off Spring Mountain—continuous sidewalks, frequent plazas.*

## Water Street District (Henderson)

> **Scope:** Historic main street lined with breweries, taverns, and restaurants; venues sit within a few blocks—ideal for a car-free hop.

### Core stretch & walkability
- **Addresses 20 S → ~203 S Water St** are within ~0.2–0.3 miles end-to-end (~5–10 min walk), with crosswalks and frequent storefronts. Featured spots below all sit on this spine.

### Picks (Breweries & Bars)
- **Lovelady Brewing Company** — family-run taproom; *20 S Water St.*  
- **Mojave Brewing Company** — beer & house cider; *107 S Water St #100.*  
- **Sticks Tavern** — chef-driven sports bar next to the Silver Knights practice facility; *203 S Water St #100.*  
- **BLVD Grille (The Pass Casino)** — cocktails + all-day menu; *46 S Water St.*  
- **Biscuits & Bourbon** — comfort food, brunch to late; *~109 S Water St.*  

### Picks (Coffee/Quick Bite)
- **Public Works Coffee Bar** — local café with patio; *Water St Plaza area.*  

### Sample walk (≈25–35 min total, excluding stops)
- Start **Lovelady Brewing (20 S)** → **BLVD Grille (46 S)** (3–5 min) → **Mojave Brewing (107 S)** (4–6 min) → **Biscuits & Bourbon (≈109 S)** (1–3 min) → **Sticks Tavern (203 S)** (5–7 min).  

*Flat sidewalks, closely spaced doors—easy out-and-back hop.*
